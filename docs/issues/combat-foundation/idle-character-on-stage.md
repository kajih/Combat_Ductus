## What to build

Integrate the Stage/camera setup with the Character rig — place one idle Character (one Body Type, placeholder face, idle arm/leg pose) correctly scaled and positioned on the Stage. Completes M0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The composited Character appears on the Stage at a plausible scale and ground position
- [ ] Camera framing shows the full Character without cropping
- [ ] Running the app (native and wasm builds) shows the idle Character on the Stage with no input, combat, or networking involved — this is M0's completion criterion
