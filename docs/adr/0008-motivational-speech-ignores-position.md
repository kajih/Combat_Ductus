# Motivational Speech ignores range and Facing, gated by conditions instead of positioning

ADR 0004 established positioning as the only defense in this game — the sole way to avoid an attack is to move out of its range. Motivational Speech is a deliberate, one-off exception to that: it deals 1 point of damage regardless of distance or Facing, so there is no way to dodge it by moving once it's thrown. To keep that from being a free win-button, it isn't balanced by range or a telegraph like Punch and Kick — it's balanced by whether the Character is even allowed to use it right now. Casting it requires the opponent to be farther away than a minimum range — so, unintuitively, this un-dodgeable attack can only be *thrown* from a distance, not up close, which also keeps it from doubling as a point-blank panic button. It's further gated by a cooldown (so it can't be spammed) and a recent-damage lockout, e.g. unusable for some seconds after last being hit (so a Character being pressured can't lean on it as an escape). Exact thresholds (the minimum range, the cooldown length, the lockout window) are tuning, deferred for now; what's fixed here is that this Special's balance levers are distance-to-cast and availability, not the damage-dealing range itself — the first attack in the game where that's true.
