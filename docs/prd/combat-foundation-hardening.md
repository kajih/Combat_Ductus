# Combat Foundation Hardening: Second-Pass Fixes & Features

## Problem Statement

After M0/M1 shipped a real two-player networked duel (real second-client control, Jump, Motivational Speech, restart), direct playtesting surfaced several rough edges that undermine trust in the multiplayer experience: a stray held-movement key can silently carry into a fresh Match after a restart; Punch and Kick have no pacing at all (button-mash spam is a fully viable strategy); nobody connecting to a Match can tell whether they're actually playing or just watching; a player who disconnects mid-fight leaves their opponent stuck fighting a helpless, undefended Character with no way to actually finish the encounter; anyone at all - including a spectator with no stake in the outcome - can force-restart a Match; and the Motivational Speech speech bubble is sometimes unreadable against the Stage backdrop. None of these break the core loop, but together they make the game feel unfinished and, in the forfeit/restart-permission cases, exploitable in ways that undermine fair play between two real people in the same room.

## Solution

Harden the M0/M1 combat/networking foundation with six focused fixes, uncovered via direct playtesting and fully designed through a structured design-review interview afterward:

- Fix held-movement leaking across a Match restart.
- Give Punch and Kick a shared pacing mechanic (attack cooldown) instead of unlimited spam, using a single cooldown timestamp per Character where Punch's threshold is half of Kick's.
- Tell every connected client which player slot (or spectator status) it holds, closing a real wire-protocol gap: the server already knows the answer internally but never tells the client.
- Turn a real player's mid-Match disconnect into a forfeit win for the remaining real player, instead of leaving their opponent's Character a stationary punching bag with no resolution.
- Restrict the restart control to the two real players, closing off a spectator's ability to force-restart a Match the players haven't finished reading yet.
- Fix the Motivational Speech speech bubble's readability using the same background-panel technique the Health HUD already established for the identical problem.

Each fix's design decisions were resolved through direct interrogation of open branches (see Implementation Decisions) rather than left to the implementer to improvise, matching this project's existing PRD/ADR-driven decision-making discipline.

## User Stories

1. As a player, I want my Character to stop moving the instant a Match restarts, so that a movement key I was holding when I won doesn't carry into the new Match as an unwanted, un-owned key-down.
2. As a player, I want this to hold even if I released the key while the Match-Ended screen was showing, so that I'm not relying on lucky timing to avoid the bug.
3. As a player, I want both Characters' held movement reset on restart, not just the winner's, so that the loser doesn't inherit the same bug from their own last-held key.
4. As a player, I want Punch and Kick to have a cooldown, so that button-mashing a single attack isn't a viable, unlimited-damage strategy.
5. As a player, I want Punch to be thrown at a faster cadence than Kick, so that the moveset has some pacing texture instead of every attack feeling identical.
6. As a player, I want a Kick I just threw to also delay my next Punch (and vice versa), so that the cooldown reflects "how recently did I attack at all," not two entirely separate, uncoupled timers.
7. As a player, I want a Punch/Kick thrown during cooldown to produce no damage and no visible animation, so that I get clear (if minimal) feedback that the attack didn't go out, rather than seeing a swing that mysteriously does nothing.
8. As a developer, I want the attack-cooldown state to live on `CharacterState` alongside Special's existing cooldown fields, so that a Match restart resets it for free without new reset code, the same way Special's cooldown already does.
9. As a player joining a Match, I want to know immediately whether I'm Player 1, Player 2, or spectating, so that I understand my own role without having to guess from what I can and can't control.
10. As a developer, I want the server to tell each connection its assigned slot directly, once, right after that connection is accepted, so that the client has an authoritative answer instead of inferring it.
11. As a developer, I want server-to-client messages to go through one self-describing wire type, so that adding this new message kind doesn't rely on fragile assumptions about message ordering.
12. As a player, I want to see a persistent on-screen indicator of my role throughout the Match, so that I don't have to re-derive it from memory if I look away and back.
13. As a developer, I want a spectator connection to be distinguishable from a real player at the protocol level, so that a future character-select screen (and any other player-only UI) can gate on it correctly.
14. As a player, I want my opponent's real disconnect during an active Match to end the Match in my favor, so that I'm not left fighting a Character nobody is controlling with no way to actually finish the encounter.
15. As a player, I want the Match-Ended screen to say my opponent disconnected when that's what happened, so that a forfeit win doesn't read identically to a real knockout.
16. As a developer, I want a disconnect to only trigger a forfeit when both slots were held by real connected players at the moment of disconnect, so that the stationary Idle Opponent is never mistakenly declared a "winner" nobody actually played.
17. As a player, I want only the two real players to be able to restart a Match, so that a spectator with no stake in the outcome can't force a restart before we've finished reading the result.
18. As a developer, I want a spectator's `RequestRestart` to be silently ignored, the same way its movement/attack input already is, so that rejection behavior is consistent across every kind of spectator input.
19. As a player, I want the Motivational Speech speech bubble to be legible no matter where on the Stage I'm standing, so that the Special's flavor text is never functionally invisible.
20. As a developer, I want the speech bubble's contrast fix to reuse the Health HUD's existing background-panel technique, so that the client's text overlays are visually consistent with each other.

## Implementation Decisions

**Module boundaries and ownership:**

- **`combat`** (deepened, unchanged in kind - still pure Rust, no Bevy/networking dependency, the sole carrier of unit tests): gains a single shared attack-cooldown timestamp per `CharacterState` (not independent Punch/Kick timers), checked inside `apply_attack` alongside the existing range/airborne checks for Punch and Kick. A Punch requires half of Kick's cooldown duration to have elapsed since the more recent of the last Punch or Kick; a Kick requires the full duration. Either attack updates the same timestamp, so either one gates the other - throwing a Kick delays the next Punch too, not just future Kicks. A cooldown-gated attempt is rejected with no animation started at all (unlike the existing out-of-range/airborne whiff, which still plays the swing) - deliberately simpler than an ideal "distinct partial-swing" animation, which is left as a future refinement outside this PRD's scope. `combat` also gains a win-reason distinction between a health-depleted win and a forfeit win, and a method that lets a caller (`server_net`) declare a forfeit winner directly - without going through `apply_attack`'s damage path - while still respecting the existing `has_ended()` guard (a forfeit can't overwrite an already-decided Match). This keeps `combat` the sole owner of "is the Match over, who won, and why," matching ADR 0005's server-authoritative design: `server_net` orchestrates *when* to ask, `combat` alone decides the answer.
- **`net_protocol`** (deepened, unchanged in kind - plain serde types, no I/O): `MatchStatus::Ended` gains the win reason alongside the winner. A new small wire type wraps every server-to-client message (the existing per-tick snapshot, plus a new one-time slot-assignment message) behind one self-describing, tagged shape, replacing today's implicit assumption that every incoming message is a bare snapshot.
- **`server_net`** (thin orchestration, unchanged in kind - still no business logic of its own beyond wiring inputs to `combat` and outputs to the network): sends the new one-time slot message to a connection right after `assign_slot` runs, before that connection starts forwarding the shared broadcast. On a connection's disconnect, if the *other* slot is also held by a real connected client, calls `combat`'s forfeit method instead of only resetting `HeldMovement` as it does today; if the other slot is the unclaimed Idle Opponent, only today's `HeldMovement` reset happens, with no forfeit. The `RequestRestart` handler resets `HeldMovement` for *both* players unconditionally when a restart is honored, and now also checks that the sending connection actually holds a player slot (rejecting a spectator's request) before honoring it at all. The existing single shared broadcast (`step_simulation` serializes one payload per tick for every connection) is unaffected in kind - only the payload's own shape changes to go through the new wrapper type.
- **`client_net`**: the client's parsed-event type gains a new variant carrying the connection's own role, produced by parsing the new wrapper type instead of assuming every message is a bare snapshot.
- **A new, small client-only module** (rendering-only, mirroring the Health HUD's existing shape - a resource holding the latest known value, an `OnEnter`/`Update`/`OnExit` system trio scoped to the Match state): stores the role delivered by the one-time message and renders a persistent, always-visible label ("Player 1" / "Player 2" / "Spectating") for the whole Match.
- **`match_end_screen`**: reads the new win-reason field and renders a distinct message for a forfeit win vs. a health-depleted win.
- **`match_characters`**: the existing speech-bubble entity gains a background panel behind its text, reusing the Health HUD's already-established "solid panel behind text guarantees contrast regardless of backdrop" technique - a contained change to existing spawn logic, not a new module.

**Cross-cutting decisions:**

- The forfeit-on-disconnect check and the restart-permission check both hinge on the same underlying fact - whether a connection holds a real player slot vs. is a spectator - which `server_net`'s existing per-connection slot tracking (from the prior second-client-controls-player-two work) already exposes; neither new behavior needs new connection-tracking machinery of its own.
- The attack-cooldown state, the win-reason field, and the slot-assignment message are all designed to require no additional reset logic on Match restart beyond what already exists (`MatchState::new()` already producing entirely fresh `CharacterState`s covers the cooldown and win-reason; the slot-assignment message is sent once per *connection*, not per Match, so a restart never needs to resend it).

## Testing Decisions

- Only external behavior is tested, never internal representation - e.g. a cooldown test asserts whether an attack call returns landed/rejected and what a subsequent snapshot shows, not the literal tick-count fields backing it, matching this project's existing test style throughout `combat` and `server_net`.
- **`combat`**: extended with unit tests, following the exact style of its existing Special-cooldown coverage (`special_is_gated_by_cooldown` is the direct precedent) - covering the Punch/Kick cross-blocking cooldown (a Kick delaying the next Punch and vice versa), the cooldown state surviving/resetting correctly across `advance_tick`, the forfeit-win method (declaring a winner directly, and correctly refusing to overwrite an already-decided Match via the existing `has_ended()` guard), and the win-reason value ending up correct for both a health-depleted win and a forfeit win.
- **`net_protocol`**: extended with round-trip serialization tests for the new wrapper type and the extended `MatchStatus::Ended`, following the exact style of its existing `state_snapshot_round_trips`/`ended_match_status_round_trips_with_winner` tests.
- **`server_net`**: extended with integration tests over a real WebSocket connection, following the exact style already established throughout this module (e.g. `jump_input_sends_player_one_airborne_and_they_land_again`, `player_two_reverts_to_idle_when_its_connection_disconnects`) - covering rapid repeated Punch/Kick presses respecting cooldown, a connection receiving its slot message immediately after connecting, a real disconnect mid-Match producing a forfeit win only when both slots were real players, and a spectator's `RequestRestart` having no effect.
- The new client-only role-indicator module, `match_end_screen`'s forfeit-message rendering, and `match_characters`'s speech-bubble background panel are **not** unit-tested, matching this project's established, consistently-applied precedent that client rendering is verified by running the game rather than automated - every prior client-only module (`health_hud`, `match_characters`, `match_end_screen` itself) has followed this same rule.

## Out of Scope

- `makefile-build-targets.md` - filed alongside these issues from the same playtesting/review session, but it's dev tooling with no gameplay/networking surface, unrelated to this PRD's actual module changes.
- `character-select/pick-character-on-connect-screen.md` - raised in the same session, but remains a larger, still-underspecified feature (a full Roster/picker UI, a new `body_type` wire field, server-side per-slot BodyType storage) that itself now depends on this PRD's connection-identity-indicator work landing first (a spectator must never see the picker, which requires already knowing a connection's role). Tracked as its own future PRD/issue, not folded in here.
- A distinct "attempted but couldn't" partial swing animation for a cooldown-gated Punch/Kick - explicitly deferred as a future refinement when designing the cooldown mechanic; this PRD ships the simpler "no animation at all" version.
- `spectator-mode/spectator-connections.md`'s own richer spectator experience (a dedicated spectator UI/UX beyond "receives the broadcast, controls nothing") - unaffected by and independent of this PRD.
- Exact cooldown tuning values (Kick's duration, from which Punch's is derived as half) - left as placeholder constants to be tuned during/after implementation, matching every other timing constant already in `combat`.

## Further Notes

- The forfeit-win and restart-permission fixes both became meaningful only because `second-client-controls-player-two.md` made spectators and a second real player genuine, reachable connection states - neither gap existed, even in theory, back when Player 2 could only ever be the stationary Idle Opponent. Worth remembering that shipping real multiplayer surfaces this whole class of "who's actually allowed to do what" question, and more of it may still be lurking (e.g. spectator-mode's own future UX work, once it exists, may reopen some of these same questions from a different angle).
- The new wrapper wire type is a genuinely new piece of protocol, not just an addition to existing types - it's the first time this project has needed more than one kind of server-to-client message. Worth treating its introduction carefully in review, since every future server-to-client message addition will follow whatever precedent this one sets.
- All of this was resolved through a structured design-review interview after direct playtesting following the M0/M1 milestone, not through a single upfront design pass - several of the "Decided" points above (especially the cooldown mechanic's exact shape, and the forfeit's scoping to real-player-vs-Idle-Opponent) went through at least one wrong initial framing that got corrected during that review. The issue files themselves (`docs/issues/combat-foundation/*.md` for each of the six items above) carry the full reasoning trail, not just the final decision.
